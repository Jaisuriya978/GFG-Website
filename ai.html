AI Fundamentals

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think, learn, and solve problems.

Narrow AI

Designed to perform a specific task (e.g., chatbots, face recognition).

General AI

A hypothetical AI that can perform any intellectual task like humans.

Super AI

AI that surpasses human intelligence (theoretical concept).

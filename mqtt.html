MQTT Protocol

Lightweight messaging protocol for IoT devices

What is MQTT?

MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport) is a lightweight, publish-subscribe messaging protocol designed for low-bandwidth, high-latency, or unreliable networks, commonly used in IoT applications.

Key Features

  • Publish/Subscribe messaging model
  • Lightweight and efficient for low-power devices
  • Supports Quality of Service (QoS) levels
  • Works over TCP/IP networks
  • Designed for IoT, mobile devices, and sensors

MQTT Architecture

MQTT uses a broker to manage messages between devices. Devices are categorized as:

  • Publisher: Sends messages to a topic
  • Subscriber: Receives messages from subscribed topics
  • Broker: Central server that routes messages

Applications of MQTT in IoT

  • Smart homes and appliances
  • Industrial IoT monitoring and control
  • Healthcare and wearable devices
  • Connected vehicles and telemetry
  • Remote sensors and environmental monitoring

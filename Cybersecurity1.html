Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Protecting systems, networks, and data from digital attacks

Security Domain

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, servers, mobile devices, networks, and data from malicious attacks, unauthorized access, and damage.

CIA Triad

  • Confidentiality – Prevent unauthorized access
  • Integrity – Ensure data accuracy and consistency
  • Availability – Ensure systems are accessible when needed

Common Cyber Threats

  • Malware (Virus, Worm, Trojan)
  • Phishing Attacks
  • Ransomware
  • Man-in-the-Middle Attacks
  • DDoS Attacks

Example: Phishing Email

Subject: Urgent! Account Verification Needed

Dear User,
Click the link below to verify your account immediately
or your account will be suspended.

http://fakebank-login.com

⚠️ This is a phishing attempt designed to steal credentials.

Security Measures

  • Strong Passwords
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
  • Firewalls
  • Antivirus Software
  • Regular Updates

Real-world Applications

Online Banking, E-commerce, Cloud Platforms, Social Media, Government Systems

Cybersecurity Career Roles

  • Security Analyst
  • Ethical Hacker
  • Security Engineer
  • Cybersecurity Consultant
  • Incident Responder

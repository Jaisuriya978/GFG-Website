Prototyping

Turning ideas into interactive experiences

What is Prototyping?

Prototyping is the process of creating interactive representations of a product to test functionality, usability, and user flow before development.

Why Prototyping is Important

  • Validates ideas early
  • Reduces development cost
  • Improves user experience
  • Encourages stakeholder feedback

Types of Prototypes

  • Low-Fidelity – Paper sketches, wireframes
  • Mid-Fidelity – Clickable layouts
  • High-Fidelity – Realistic UI interactions

Common Prototyping Tools

  • Figma
  • Adobe XD
  • Sketch
  • InVision
  • Axure

Prototyping Best Practices

  • Start simple and iterate
  • Focus on user flows
  • Test with real users
  • Use feedback effectively

Prototyping in Design Thinking

Prototyping belongs to the Prototype and Test stages of Design Thinking.

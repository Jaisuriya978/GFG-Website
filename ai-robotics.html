Artificial Intelligence in Robotics

How AI makes robots smart, adaptive, and autonomous

What is AI in Robotics?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) in robotics allows robots to perceive, learn, reason, and make decisions without human intervention. AI-powered robots can adapt to their environment and improve performance over time.

Key AI Technologies Used in Robotics

  • Machine Learning: Enables robots to learn from data.
  • Computer Vision: Helps robots recognize objects and faces.
  • Natural Language Processing: Allows human-robot interaction.
  • Deep Learning: Used for image and speech recognition.
  • Reinforcement Learning: Robots learn via rewards and penalties.

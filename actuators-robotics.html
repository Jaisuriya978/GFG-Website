Actuators in Robotics

Actuators convert electrical signals into physical movement

DC Motor

Provides continuous rotational motion.

Servo Motor

Used for precise angular movement.

Stepper Motor

Moves in discrete steps for accuracy.

Hydraulic Actuator

Uses fluid pressure for heavy movement.

Pneumatic Actuator

Uses compressed air for motion control.

Thermal Actuator

Uses heat to trigger mechanical action.

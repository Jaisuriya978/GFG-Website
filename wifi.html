Wi-Fi

Wireless technology for local area networking in IoT and smart devices

What is Wi-Fi?

Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that allows devices like microcontrollers, sensors, smartphones, and computers to communicate over a local area network (LAN) using radio waves.

Key Features of Wi-Fi

  • Wireless connectivity using radio frequencies (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands)
  • Supports high-speed data transfer
  • Enables multiple devices to connect simultaneously
  • Secured using protocols like WPA2/WPA3
  • Widely used for IoT, smart homes, and industrial systems

Wi-Fi Standards

  • 802.11a – Up to 54 Mbps, 5 GHz
  • 802.11b – Up to 11 Mbps, 2.4 GHz
  • 802.11g – Up to 54 Mbps, 2.4 GHz
  • 802.11n – Up to 600 Mbps, 2.4/5 GHz
  • 802.11ac – Up to several Gbps, 5 GHz
  • 802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6) – Enhanced throughput and efficiency

Applications of Wi-Fi in IoT

  • Smart homes: lights, thermostats, and security systems
  • Industrial IoT: monitoring and automation systems
  • Wearables and healthcare devices
  • Wireless sensors and data logging
  • Robotics and drones communication
  • Cloud-connected IoT devices

Introduction to Robotics

Learn what robotics is, its components, types, and applications

What is Robotics?

Robotics is a branch of engineering and science that involves the design, construction, operation, and use of robots.

Main Components of a Robot

  • Controller (Microcontroller / Microprocessor)
  • Sensors
  • Actuators (Motors)
  • Power Supply
  • Mechanical Structure

Types of Robots

  • Industrial Robots
  • Mobile Robots
  • Humanoid Robots
  • Autonomous Robots

Applications of Robotics

  • Manufacturing
  • Medical Surgery
  • Space Exploration
  • Defense & Security
  • Artificial Intelligence

Real-World Usage of Robotics

  • Robots are used in various fields such as manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture, and space exploration.
  • Examples include robotic arms in factories, surgical robots in hospitals, and rovers on Mars.
  • Robots are also used in education, entertainment, and domestic applications.
  • Robots are increasingly being integrated into everyday life, from home assistants to industrial automation.

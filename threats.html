Cybersecurity Threats

Common Threats and Risks in the Cyber World

Overview

Cybersecurity threats are malicious acts that seek to damage data, steal sensitive information, or disrupt digital systems. Understanding these threats helps individuals and organizations strengthen their security posture.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

  • Malware: Malicious software such as viruses, worms, trojans, and ransomware.
  • Phishing: Fraudulent attempts to obtain sensitive information via email or messaging.
  • Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) Attacks: Intercepting communication between two parties to steal data.
  • DDoS Attacks: Distributed Denial-of-Service attacks overwhelm systems, causing downtime.
  • SQL Injection: Exploiting vulnerabilities in web applications to access databases.
  • Password Attacks: Brute force, dictionary, and credential stuffing attacks.
  • Insider Threats: Threats originating from employees or trusted individuals.
  • Zero-Day Exploits: Attacks exploiting unpatched software vulnerabilities.

Best Practices to Mitigate Threats

  • Use strong passwords and multi-factor authentication
  • Regularly update software and patch vulnerabilities
  • Educate users about phishing and social engineering
  • Implement firewalls, antivirus, and intrusion detection systems
  • Regularly backup data and enforce access control policies

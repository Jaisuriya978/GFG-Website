Deque (Double Ended Queue)

Insertion and deletion from both ends of the queue

What is a Deque?

A Deque (Double Ended Queue) is a linear data structure that allows insertion and deletion of elements from both front and rear ends.

It is more flexible than a normal queue.

Types of Deque

  • Input Restricted Deque – Insertion at one end, deletion at both ends
  • Output Restricted Deque – Deletion at one end, insertion at both ends

Basic Operations

  • InsertFront – Insert element at the front
  • InsertRear – Insert element at the rear
  • DeleteFront – Remove element from the front
  • DeleteRear – Remove element from the rear
  • GetFront / GetRear – View elements at both ends

Applications of Deque

  • Sliding window problems
  • Palindrome checking
  • Undo and redo operations
  • Task scheduling
  • Maintaining monotonic queues

Advantages of Deque

  • Efficient insertion and deletion
  • Can act as both stack and queue
  • Flexible data access

ESP32

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth-enabled microcontroller for advanced IoT projects

What is ESP32?

ESP32 is a powerful, low-cost microcontroller with integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. It is widely used in IoT, wearable electronics, and smart devices for real-time data processing and wireless communication.

Features of ESP32

  • Dual-core or single-core 32-bit processor
  • Built-in Wi-Fi (802.11 b/g/n) and Bluetooth 4.2 / BLE
  • Multiple GPIO pins for sensors, actuators, and peripherals
  • Supports ADC, DAC, SPI, I2C, UART, and PWM interfaces
  • Low-power sleep modes for battery-operated projects
  • Compatible with Arduino IDE, MicroPython, and ESP-IDF

Applications of ESP32 in IoT

  • Home automation and smart lighting systems
  • Wearable devices and health monitoring
  • Wireless sensor networks
  • Robotics and drones
  • Industrial IoT and predictive maintenance
  • IoT data logging and cloud integration

DL Fundamentals

What is Deep Learning?

Deep Learning (DL) is a subset of Machine Learning that uses artificial neural networks with many layers to model complex patterns in data.

Neural Networks

Neural networks consist of input layers, hidden layers, and output layers. Each neuron applies a weight, bias, and activation function.

Output = Activation(Wx + b)

ANN

Artificial Neural Networks used for general tasks.

CNN

Convolutional Neural Networks mainly used for image processing.

RNN

Recurrent Neural Networks used for sequential data.

Advanced Deep Learning Models

  • LSTM & GRU
  • Autoencoders
  • GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks)
  • Transformers

Applications of Deep Learning

  • Computer Vision
  • Speech Recognition
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Autonomous Vehicles

Advantages & Challenges

  • High accuracy for complex tasks
  • Requires large datasets
  • High computational cost

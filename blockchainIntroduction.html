Introduction to Blockchain

A Beginner’s Guide to Blockchain Technology

What is Blockchain?

Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that enables secure, transparent, and tamper-proof recording of transactions across a decentralized network.

Why Blockchain is Important?

  • Eliminates the need for intermediaries
  • Enhances data security and trust
  • Improves transparency and traceability
  • Reduces fraud and operational costs

Core Components of Blockchain

  • Block: Stores transaction data
  • Hash: Unique cryptographic identifier
  • Chain: Links blocks securely
  • Nodes: Network participants
  • Consensus: Agreement mechanism

Consensus Mechanisms

  • Proof of Work (PoW)
  • Proof of Stake (PoS)
  • Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)
  • Proof of Authority (PoA)

Applications of Blockchain

  • Cryptocurrencies
  • Smart Contracts
  • Supply Chain Management
  • Healthcare Records
  • Digital Identity

Advantages & Limitations

Advantages: Transparency, decentralization, immutability

Limitations: Scalability issues, energy consumption, regulation challenges

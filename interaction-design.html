Interaction Design

Designing meaningful user interactions

What is Interaction Design?

Interaction Design (IxD) focuses on how users interact with digital products and ensures those interactions are intuitive, efficient, and enjoyable.

Goals of Interaction Design

  • Make interactions intuitive
  • Reduce user effort
  • Provide clear feedback
  • Improve usability and satisfaction

Key Elements of Interaction Design

  • Words – Labels, instructions
  • Visuals – Icons, buttons
  • Physical objects – Touch, gestures
  • Time – Animations, transitions
  • Behavior – System response

Interaction Design Principles

  • Consistency
  • Feedback
  • Affordance
  • Visibility
  • Constraints

Examples of Interaction Design

  • Button hover effects
  • Form validation messages
  • Swipe gestures
  • Loading animations

Interaction Design vs UI Design

UI Design focuses on how things look, while Interaction Design focuses on how things work.

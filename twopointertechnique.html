Two Pointer Technique

Efficient traversal using two indices

What is Two Pointer Technique?

The Two Pointer Technique uses two indices to iterate through a data structure to reduce time complexity.

It is commonly used in arrays and strings.

Types of Two Pointer Technique

  • Opposite Direction Pointers
  • Same Direction (Fast & Slow)

How It Works

  • Initialize two pointers
  • Move pointers based on condition
  • Stop when pointers meet or condition satisfied

Common Problems

  • Pair with given sum
  • Remove duplicates from sorted array
  • Reverse array/string
  • Detect cycle (Floyd’s Algorithm)

Advantages & Limitations

  • Reduces time complexity
  • Uses constant space
  • Often requires sorted data
  • Not applicable to all problems

Bitcoin (BTC)

The First Decentralized Digital Currency

What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency introduced in 2008 by an anonymous person or group known as Satoshi Nakamoto. It allows peer-to-peer transactions without intermediaries like banks.

Why Bitcoin Was Created

  • Remove dependence on banks
  • Prevent double spending
  • Provide transparency and security
  • Enable global payments

How Bitcoin Works

  • Transactions are grouped into blocks
  • Blocks are linked using cryptographic hashes
  • Miners validate transactions
  • Blockchain stores immutable records

Consensus Mechanism

  • Uses Proof of Work (PoW)
  • Miners solve cryptographic puzzles
  • Secures the network

Bitcoin Mining

Mining is the process of validating transactions and adding blocks to the blockchain. Miners are rewarded with newly created bitcoins.

Block Reward + Transaction Fees

Bitcoin Supply

  • Maximum supply: 21 million BTC
  • Halving occurs every 4 years
  • Scarcity increases value

Advantages of Bitcoin

  • Decentralized and censorship-resistant
  • Highly secure
  • Limited supply
  • Global accessibility

Limitations

  • High energy consumption
  • Slow transaction speed
  • Price volatility

Bitcoin Use Cases

  • Digital gold
  • Store of value
  • Cross-border payments

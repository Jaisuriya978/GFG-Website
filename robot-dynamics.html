What is Robot Dynamics?

Robot dynamics deals with how forces and torques produce motion in robots. It is essential for robot control, simulation, and safety.

Robot Dynamic Equation

τ = M(q)q̈ + C(q, q̇)q̇ + G(q)

This equation models robot joint motion including inertia, velocity effects, and gravity.

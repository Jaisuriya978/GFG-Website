Careers in Robotics

Explore exciting job roles and career paths in robotics

🤖 Robotics Engineer

Design, build, and test robotic systems.

  • Skills: C++, Python, ROS
  • Education: B.E / B.Tech
  • Experience: 0–2 years

🧠 AI Robotics Developer

Develop intelligent algorithms for robots.

  • Skills: ML, Deep Learning, Python
  • Education: B.Tech / M.Tech
  • Experience: 2–5 years

🏭 Robotics System Architect

Design large-scale robotic solutions.

  • Skills: System Design, ROS2, Automation
  • Education: M.Tech / PhD
  • Experience: 5+ years

Android Services

Background Processing & Lifecycle Management

What is an Android Service?

An Android Service is a component that runs in the background to perform long-running operations without a user interface.

  • Runs in background
  • No direct UI interaction
  • Performs continuous or scheduled tasks

Types of Services

  • Foreground Service – Displays notification (Music Player)
  • Background Service – Runs silently
  • Bound Service – Binds with Activity

Service Lifecycle

onCreate()
onStartCommand()
onBind()
onDestroy()

Real-World Usage

  • Music Streaming Apps
  • File Download Managers
  • Background Data Sync
  • Location Tracking Apps

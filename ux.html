User Experience (UX) Design

Designing meaningful and user-centered experiences

What is UX Design?

User Experience (UX) Design focuses on improving the overall experience users have while interacting with a product, system, or service.

UX Design Process

  • User Research
  • Persona Creation
  • Information Architecture
  • Wireframing
  • Prototyping
  • Usability Testing

Key UX Principles

  • User-centric design
  • Accessibility
  • Consistency
  • Feedback & Usability
  • Simplicity

UX Research Methods

  • User Interviews
  • Surveys & Questionnaires
  • Usability Testing
  • A/B Testing

UX Design Tools

  • Figma
  • Adobe XD
  • Sketch
  • Miro

UX vs UI

  • UX focuses on experience & flow
  • UI focuses on visuals & interaction

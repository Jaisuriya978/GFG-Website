System Design

Learn how to design scalable, reliable, and efficient systems

What is System Design?

System Design is the process of defining the architecture, components, modules, interfaces, and data flow of a system to meet specific requirements.

Key Concepts

  • Scalability (Horizontal & Vertical)
  • Reliability and Fault Tolerance
  • Availability and Consistency
  • Load Balancing
  • Caching and Databases

Why System Design Matters?

  • Handles millions of users efficiently
  • Reduces system failures
  • Improves performance and scalability
  • Crucial for backend & interview preparation

Real-World Examples

  • Designing a URL Shortener
  • Chat Application (WhatsApp)
  • Video Streaming Platform (YouTube)
  • E-commerce System (Amazon)

Cyber-Physical Systems in Cybersecurity

Understanding Security Challenges and Protections for CPS

Overview

Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) integrate computation, networking, and physical processes. Due to their interconnection and real-time nature, they are vulnerable to cyber-attacks that can impact physical safety.

Security Challenges in CPS

  • Real-time Constraints: Attacks must be mitigated without delaying critical operations.
  • Physical Impact: Cyber attacks can cause physical damage to systems (e.g., industrial robots, power grids).
  • Heterogeneous Devices: Diverse sensors, actuators, and embedded devices increase attack surface.
  • Communication Vulnerabilities: Wireless networks and IoT devices can be intercepted or spoofed.
  • Complexity: Large-scale CPS networks are difficult to monitor entirely.

Security Measures for CPS

  • Network segmentation and isolation for critical components
  • Encryption of data in transit between sensors, actuators, and servers
  • Authentication and access control for devices and users
  • Real-time monitoring and anomaly detection
  • Redundancy and fault-tolerant design to prevent catastrophic failures
  • Regular security audits and patching of embedded systems

Examples of CPS in Cybersecurity Context

  • Smart Grid security against power manipulation attacks
  • Autonomous vehicle protection against hijacking or spoofing
  • Industrial Control Systems (ICS) cybersecurity
  • Healthcare CPS securing medical devices

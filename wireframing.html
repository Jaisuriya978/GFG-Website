Wireframing

Structuring ideas before visual design

What is Wireframing?

Wireframing is the process of creating low-fidelity layouts that represent the structure and functionality of a screen without focusing on colors or visuals.

Purpose of Wireframes

  • Defines layout and content hierarchy
  • Clarifies navigation and user flow
  • Helps align stakeholders early
  • Saves time during UI design

Types of Wireframes

  • Low-Fidelity – Sketches, grayscale layouts
  • Mid-Fidelity – Detailed structure, placeholders
  • High-Fidelity – Close to final UI (rare)

Common Wireframing Tools

  • Figma
  • Balsamiq
  • Adobe XD
  • Sketch
  • Pen & Paper ✏️

Wireframing Best Practices

  • Focus on functionality, not visuals
  • Use placeholders for content
  • Design mobile-first
  • Get early feedback

Wireframing vs Prototyping

Wireframes define structure, while prototypes focus on interaction and user experience.

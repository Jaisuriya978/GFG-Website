Bluetooth

Wireless communication protocol for short-range IoT and smart device connections

What is Bluetooth?

Bluetooth is a short-range wireless communication technology that allows devices like microcontrollers, smartphones, computers, and sensors to exchange data over a limited distance, typically up to 100 meters.

Key Features of Bluetooth

  • Short-range wireless communication (typically 10–100 meters)
  • Low-power consumption for IoT devices
  • Supports point-to-point and multipoint connections
  • Used for data, audio, and control signal transmission
  • Operates in the 2.4 GHz ISM radio band

Bluetooth Versions

  • Bluetooth 1.0 & 1.1 – Basic data rates up to 721 kbps
  • Bluetooth 2.0 + EDR – Enhanced Data Rate up to 3 Mbps
  • Bluetooth 3.0 + HS – High speed using Wi-Fi for data transfer
  • Bluetooth 4.0 – Low energy (BLE) for IoT devices
  • Bluetooth 5.0 & 5.1 – Increased range, speed, and reliability

Applications of Bluetooth in IoT

  • Wireless communication between microcontrollers and sensors
  • Wearable devices and health monitors
  • Smart home automation devices
  • Robotics and remote control systems
  • Audio streaming and wireless headsets
  • Beacon technology and proximity sensors

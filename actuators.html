IoT Actuators

Devices that perform actions based on sensor data in IoT systems

What are Actuators?

Actuators are IoT devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions. They respond to data received from sensors or controllers to perform tasks like movement, switching, or control operations.

Types of IoT Actuators

  • Electric Actuators: Operate devices like motors, solenoids, and relays using electricity.
  • Pneumatic Actuators: Use compressed air to create mechanical motion.
  • Hydraulic Actuators: Use pressurized fluid for heavy-duty movement.
  • Thermal Actuators: Use heat to trigger mechanical action.

How Actuators Work

  1. Receive a control signal from a sensor, microcontroller, or cloud system.
  2. Convert the signal into physical motion or mechanical operation.
  3. Perform actions such as turning on a motor, opening a valve, or switching a device.

Applications of Actuators in IoT

  • Automated doors and windows
  • Robotics and industrial automation
  • Smart home devices (lights, HVAC, fans)
  • Medical devices (pumps, prosthetics)
  • Agriculture (irrigation systems, greenhouse ventilation)

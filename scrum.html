Scrum Framework

Popular Agile Framework for Product Development

What is Scrum?

Scrum is an Agile framework used to manage and develop complex software products through iterative and incremental development.

Scrum Pillars

  • Transparency
  • Inspection
  • Adaptation

Scrum Roles

  • Product Owner
  • Scrum Master
  • Development Team

Scrum Artifacts

  • Product Backlog
  • Sprint Backlog
  • Increment

Scrum Events

  • Sprint
  • Sprint Planning
  • Daily Scrum
  • Sprint Review
  • Sprint Retrospective

Sprint

A Sprint is a time-boxed iteration (usually 1–4 weeks) where a potentially shippable product increment is developed.

Advantages of Scrum

  • Quick delivery of features
  • High transparency
  • Better team collaboration
  • Continuous feedback

Scrum vs Agile

  • Agile is a philosophy
  • Scrum is an Agile framework
  • Scrum follows fixed roles and ceremonies

Statistics

Mathematical Foundations for Data Analysis

What is Statistics?

Statistics is the science of collecting, organizing, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting data to make informed decisions.

Types of Statistics

  • Descriptive Statistics – Summarizes data
  • Inferential Statistics – Draws conclusions from samples

Descriptive Statistics

  • Mean
  • Median
  • Mode
  • Range
  • Variance
  • Standard Deviation

Inferential Statistics

  • Sampling
  • Probability
  • Confidence Intervals
  • Hypothesis Testing
  • Regression Analysis

Probability Basics

Probability measures the likelihood of an event occurring, ranging from 0 to 1.

Simple Example


import statistics

data = [10, 20, 30, 40, 50]
print(statistics.mean(data))
print(statistics.median(data))

Applications of Statistics

  • Business forecasting
  • Medical research
  • Economics
  • Machine Learning
  • Quality control

Why Statistics is Important

  • Helps in data-driven decisions
  • Identifies trends and patterns
  • Reduces uncertainty
  • Supports predictive modeling

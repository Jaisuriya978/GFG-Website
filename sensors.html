IoT Sensors

Devices that detect and measure physical parameters in IoT systems

What are Sensors?

Sensors are IoT devices that detect changes in the physical environment, such as temperature, motion, light, or humidity, and convert them into data that can be processed.

Types of Sensors in IoT

  • Temperature Sensors: Measure heat, e.g., DHT11, DS18B20
  • Motion Sensors: Detect movement, e.g., PIR sensor
  • Proximity Sensors: Detect objects or distance
  • Light Sensors: Measure light intensity, e.g., LDR
  • Gas Sensors: Detect gases in the environment, e.g., MQ-2, MQ-7
  • Humidity Sensors: Measure moisture in the air, e.g., DHT22
  • Pressure Sensors: Measure force or pressure, e.g., BMP180
  • Sound Sensors: Detect noise levels

Working of Sensors

  1. Sensor detects a physical change in the environment.
  2. Converts the detected change into an electrical signal.
  3. Signal is sent to a microcontroller or gateway for processing.
  4. Processed data is sent to the cloud or local device for monitoring and action.

Applications of Sensors in IoT

  • Smart homes (temperature, motion, light)
  • Healthcare monitoring (heart rate, glucose)
  • Industrial automation (pressure, vibration)
  • Agriculture (soil moisture, humidity)
  • Smart cities (air quality, traffic monitoring)

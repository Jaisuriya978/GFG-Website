Consensus Algorithms

How Decentralized Networks Agree on the Truth

What is a Consensus Algorithm?

A consensus algorithm is a mechanism that enables distributed blockchain nodes to agree on a single version of the ledger without relying on a central authority.

Why Consensus is Important

  • Prevents double spending
  • Maintains data consistency
  • Ensures network security
  • Builds trust in decentralized systems

Key Properties of Consensus Algorithms

  • Fault tolerance
  • Decentralization
  • Scalability
  • Security
  • Energy efficiency

Popular Consensus Algorithms

  • Proof of Work (PoW)
  • Proof of Stake (PoS)
  • Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)
  • Proof of Authority (PoA)
  • Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT)

Proof of Work (PoW)

  • Used by Bitcoin
  • Miners solve cryptographic puzzles
  • High energy consumption
  • Very secure but less scalable

Proof of Stake (PoS)

  • Validators stake tokens
  • Lower energy usage
  • Faster transactions
  • Used by Ethereum 2.0

Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)

  • Users vote for delegates
  • High throughput
  • More centralized

Proof of Authority (PoA)

  • Validators are pre-approved
  • Used in private blockchains
  • High efficiency

Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT)

  • Handles malicious nodes
  • Used in permissioned blockchains
  • Ensures system reliability

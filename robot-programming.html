What is Robot Programming?

Robot programming involves writing instructions that control a robot’s movement, sensing, and decision-making. It enables robots to perform tasks automatically and intelligently.

Popular Programming Languages in Robotics

  • Python: Easy and widely used in AI robotics.
  • C++: High-performance programming for ROS.
  • Java: Used in Android-based robotics.
  • MATLAB: Used for simulations and control systems.

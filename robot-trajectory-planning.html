What is Trajectory Planning?

Trajectory planning determines how a robot moves from one position to another smoothly and safely over time.

It considers position, velocity, and acceleration constraints.

Common trajectory types include linear, cubic, and logistic trajectories.

Trajectory planning is crucial for tasks like pick-and-place, navigation, and any application requiring precise motion control.

It ensures efficient, safe, and accurate robot movements in various environments.

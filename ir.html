Incident Response

Strategies and Processes to Handle Cybersecurity Incidents

What is Incident Response?

Incident Response (IR) is a structured approach for detecting, investigating, and responding to cybersecurity incidents. Effective IR minimizes damage, reduces recovery time, and prevents future attacks.

Phases of Incident Response

  • Preparation: Establish policies, tools, and team readiness.
  • Identification: Detect and determine if an event qualifies as an incident.
  • Containment: Short-term and long-term containment to prevent spread.
  • Eradication: Remove malware, unauthorized access, and vulnerabilities.
  • Recovery: Restore systems to normal operations safely.
  • Lessons Learned: Analyze incident and improve future defenses.

Common Tools for Incident Response

  • SIEM Tools (e.g., Splunk, QRadar) – Log analysis & monitoring
  • Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) – Malware and anomaly detection
  • Network Traffic Analysis – Wireshark, Zeek
  • Forensic Tools – Autopsy, FTK, EnCase
  • Incident Management Platforms – TheHive, ServiceNow

Best Practices

  • Establish a dedicated incident response team (IRT)
  • Maintain up-to-date playbooks and policies
  • Regularly conduct incident response drills
  • Ensure communication and reporting channels are clear
  • Document all incidents and actions taken for post-analysis

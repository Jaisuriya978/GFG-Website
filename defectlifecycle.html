Defect Life Cycle

Bug Life Cycle in Software Testing

What is Defect Life Cycle?

The Defect Life Cycle describes the various stages a defect (bug) goes through from identification to closure during the testing process.

Why Defect Life Cycle is Important

  • Tracks defect status clearly
  • Improves communication between testers and developers
  • Ensures defects are resolved properly
  • Improves software quality

Stages of Defect Life Cycle

  • New
  • Assigned
  • Open
  • Fixed
  • Retest
  • Verified
  • Closed
  • Reopened
  • Deferred
  • Rejected

New

Tester identifies and logs a defect for the first time.

Assigned

Defect is assigned to a developer or development team for fixing.

Open

Developer starts working on the defect.

Fixed

Developer fixes the defect and marks it as fixed.

Retest

Tester retests the defect to verify the fix.

Verified

Tester confirms that the defect is fixed successfully.

Closed

Defect is closed after successful verification.

Rejected

Defect is rejected if it is not valid or cannot be reproduced.

Deferred

Defect is postponed to a future release due to low priority.

Reopened

Defect is reopened if it still exists after fixing.

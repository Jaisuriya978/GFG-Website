Embedded Systems

Integration of hardware and software to perform dedicated functions in devices

What is an Embedded System?

An Embedded System is a combination of hardware and software designed to perform a specific function within a larger system. Unlike general-purpose computers, embedded systems are optimized for dedicated tasks.

Characteristics of Embedded Systems

  • Task-specific functionality
  • Real-time operation
  • Limited hardware resources (CPU, memory, storage)
  • High reliability and stability
  • Low power consumption
  • Embedded software tightly coupled with hardware

Components of Embedded Systems

  • Microcontroller or microprocessor
  • Memory (ROM, RAM, Flash)
  • Input devices (sensors, switches)
  • Output devices (LEDs, motors, displays)
  • Communication interfaces (UART, SPI, I2C, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth)
  • Power supply and clock circuitry

Applications of Embedded Systems

  • IoT devices and smart homes
  • Automotive systems (ECUs, ABS, infotainment)
  • Medical devices and wearable health monitors
  • Industrial automation and robotics
  • Consumer electronics (microwaves, washing machines, cameras)
  • Networking devices (routers, modems)

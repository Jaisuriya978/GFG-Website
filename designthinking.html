Design Thinking

A human-centered approach to problem solving

What is Design Thinking?

Design Thinking is a problem-solving methodology that focuses on understanding user needs, challenging assumptions, and creating innovative solutions.

Why Design Thinking Matters

  • Encourages innovation
  • Improves user satisfaction
  • Reduces product risk
  • Promotes collaboration

Five Stages of Design Thinking

  • Empathize – Understand users
  • Define – Identify the problem
  • Ideate – Generate ideas
  • Prototype – Build solutions
  • Test – Validate with users

Design Thinking in UI/UX

  • User-centered interfaces
  • Better usability and accessibility
  • Iterative design process
  • Data-driven decisions

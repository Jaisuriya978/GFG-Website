Sensors in Robotics

Sensors help robots sense and understand their environment

Motion Sensor

Detects movement in the environment.

Ultrasonic Sensor

Measures distance using sound waves.

Proximity Sensor

Detects presence of nearby objects without physical contact.

Infrared Sensor

Detects objects using infrared light.

Temperature Sensor

Measures temperature levels.

Camera Sensor

Used for vision and image processing.

What is Android Development?

Android Development is the process of creating applications for Android-powered devices using tools like Android Studio, Kotlin, and Java.

Key Concepts

  • Activities & Lifecycle
  • Fragments
  • Intents & Navigation
  • RecyclerView & UI Components
  • Data Storage (Room, SQLite, SharedPreferences)

Why Android Development Matters?

  • Largest mobile OS market share
  • Huge career opportunities
  • Open-source ecosystem
  • Used in mobiles, TVs, watches & IoT

Real-World Applications

  • Social Media Apps
  • Banking Applications
  • E-commerce Apps
  • Food Delivery Apps
